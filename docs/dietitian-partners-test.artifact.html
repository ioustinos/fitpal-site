Connecting…

What this pass covers

The dietitian partner feature (WEC-838 — Dietitian Partners epic: dietitians resell plans and order for their clients) on the dev site, from three browser windows: admin, dietitian and client. It walks every screen that shipped: admin dietitian management, the /partner portal, the client's accept and consent prompts, the dietitian discount at checkout, commission and payouts, and the two paid add-ons. The dev database holds 25 anonymized customers «Πελάτης Δοκιμής N» with real-looking orders; none is linked to a dietitian yet.

The three browser windows

You play three people at once, so you need three windows that don't share a sign-in. Every step starts with SIGNED IN AS: it names the window and the account. Keep all three open side by side for the whole pass.

How to sign in (the same in every window)

  1. Open https://dev--fitpal-order.netlify.app in that window.
  2. Click «Σύνδεση» at the top right.
  3. Click «Στείλε μου κωδικό στο email».
  4. Type the account's email and click «Στείλε μου κωδικό».
  5. Open your own Gmail (ioustinos.sarris@gmail.com). The code arrives there for every account, because they are all +aliases of your address.
  6. Type the code and click «Επαλήθευση & σύνδεση».

To check who is signed in: your name or email shows at the top right. To sign out: click it, then «Αποσύνδεση».

A step that says «SIGNED OUT» means: sign out in that window before you start.

Reaching the first screen

  • Site: https://dev--fitpal-order.netlify.app
  • Admin: /admin/partners (sidebar «Dietitians»). Portal: /partner. Referral: /ref/test-e2e-diet
  • Viva sandbox card: 4111 1111 1111 1111, any future expiry, any CVV, 3DS code 111111
  • The TEST dietitian «TEST Διαιτολόγος (E2E)» starts at 6% client discount / 6% commission with progress tracking and referral on. If you change the defaults in 1.3, read every 6% below as your number.

Looks like a fault, is not

  • Seeded orders have numbers starting SD-, not FP-. That's deliberate so they never clash with new dev orders.
  • «Ως πελάτης» / «Place order for this customer» opens a new tab (WEC-868). If Chrome says it blocked a pop-up, allow pop-ups for the dev site once and press the button again.
  • Wallet-paid orders always show 0 commission. The commission was earned when the plan was bought.
  • Cash and transfer orders show 0 commission until they're marked paid. Commission counts collected money only.
  • Ordering for a client from your own account records the order as placed by admin, not by the dietitian, because you're an admin. Part 5 uses the DIETITIAN window for that reason.

Reporting a failure

Press Fail and write what you saw: the amount, the text on screen, the client, and whether it repeats. Blocked means an earlier failure stopped you. Everything saves itself as you go, and I can read the results directly. Nothing to send.

Known gaps going in — read before you start

  • No ticket reflects the code yet. In Linear, WEC-839 (Partners DB foundation) is In Progress and WEC-840…853 are all Todo, although most of their code is on dev. Your verdicts here decide where each one goes.
  • WEC-853 (dietitian notifications) is not built. The add-on switch saves, but nothing is sent. Not covered below.
  • WEC-842 (add client) asks for an email confirmation for existing customers. What was built instead is an in-site prompt at the client's next sign-in. No email is sent.
  • WEC-849 (plans through the dietitian): in the code, the client discount applies to orders only. A plan bought through a dietitian earns commission but gets no dietitian discount.
  • Order history: the dietitian sees every order of a linked client, including orders placed before the link existed.
  • The QR image is drawn by an outside service (api.qrserver.com), which receives the referral link.
